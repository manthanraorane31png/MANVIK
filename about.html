About MANVIK

We are building a smarter way for people to discover, compare and book trusted home service professionals.

Who We Are

MANVIK is an AI-powered home service comparison and booking platform designed to make finding trusted professionals simple, transparent and convenient. Users can compare providers based on price, ratings, experience, distance and availability before making a booking.

Finding a reliable home service professional can often mean making multiple calls, checking different prices and searching through reviews.

MANVIK brings these choices together in one platform. Customers can compare professionals based on price, ratings, experience, distance and availability before making a decision.

Our goal is simple: make home service decisions more transparent, convenient and intelligent.
